For the completion of this project, the tools Audacity and Clipchamp were used.
With Audacity, new sounds were created and edited to modify the meaning of the story. The resources employed included dialogue recordings, horror and suspense music, as well as thunder and sharp impact effects, all of which provided a new atmosphere and perspective to the trailer.
Subsequently, Clipchamp was used to integrate these sounds into the original video, achieving synchronization between the audio edition and the visuals, thus enhancing coherence and the overall impact of the final result.
